Exploration Roadmap

Welcome to 30 Days of Pharmaverse. This project is a hands-on exploration of the modern clinical data science stack, designed for programmers and data scientists who want to dive deep into CDISC standards using the open-source Pharmaverse ecosystem.

Instead of a traditional course, think of this as a guided tour or a developer’s log. Each day, we tackle a specific concept-from setting up a validated environment to building complex submission-ready datasets-using industry-standard R packages like admiral, xportr, and metacore.

What You’ll Explore

This journey covers the essential toolkit for modern clinical programming:

  • SDTM & ADaM Standards: Understanding the “why” and “how” of standardized data.
  • Pharmaverse Tools: Getting hands-on with admiral, pharmaversesdtm, xportr, and more.
  • End-to-End Workflows: From raw data ingestion to FDA-compliant .xpt export.
  • Best Practices: Writing clean, traceable, and reproducible code for regulatory submissions.

The Toolkit

Prerequisites: - Comfort with R and the tidyverse - A curiosity about clinical trials and data standards
